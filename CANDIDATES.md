# Candidates — local judging

Eight candidates were judged locally against the brief in order: (1) a one-star alien review of Earth with a food compliment; (2) convincingly alien voice and a grievance worth one star; (3) complaint and compliment both hit with a funny turn; (4) one sentence within the limit, every word earning its place; (5) a fresh original angle. The field was saturated with dumplings, tacos, noodles, ramen, and "worth the invasion/abduction" turns, so candidates leaning on those were marked down.

## Selected

> One star: I fed your sourdough starter for a week, named it, then watched you bake its children into bread so good I ate two slices and filed for custody of the jar before leaving orbit.

186 characters, exactly one sentence. It has a full arc — bonding with the starter, horror at baking its children, surrender to the bread, and the custody filing as the turn — plus an explicit food compliment ("bread so good I ate two slices"), alien-tourist markers (custody bureaucracy, leaving orbit), and a fresh angle with no saturated food or invasion cliché.

## Runner-up

Popcorn as "the finest debris in this galaxy": the strongest explicit alien voice (antennae), but the compliment is more ironic and the setup reads as a list.

## Third

Birthday cake, "applauded the arson": good orbit voice, weaker grievance.

## Rejected

The remaining candidates had weaker turns, implicit compliments, or thinner alien voice.
